## Monday, December 29 2025, 7:22 PM

It's been three months since I worked on this website. I've only returned now to fill this up with some actual human thoughts.
I'm rewriting this paragraph for the fourth time now, wondering if another mind but mine will ever read this.

I don't think the goal of me writing this is perception. I don't think it even makes the list of all the things I want this to be.
But maybe, just maybe, another human is reading this, probably trying to get a grasp of who I am as a person. At least, that's what I would be doing if you ever caught me on someone's page.
Hey now, I didn't say that's wrong. You and I can try to figure me out for a bit, okay?

Lately, I've been quite sporadic in my pursuit of interests. A "flow" strikes me every now and then, and I can't stop soaring, trying to be this admired and knowledgeable version of me I have in my head.
But I know better than to think that's good. I've seen what repetition can do and how it can transform a person. I just can't help but crave that high again.

I'm the first to find the "disciplined day in my life" routines cringe and unrealistic, the first to laugh at anything motivational. But I've experienced life when you are just dialed in on one thing, to the point that nothing else matters.
Oh, I know that was very toxic. It took away from the formative years of my life. I didn't develop social skills or political awareness until much later due to how burrowing it was.
But I can't help but wonder if I can recreate that with things in balance. Where I'm living without guilt about wasted potential. Not worrying about years I lost not being my best.

What worries me is that I've had these exact thoughts for over a year now. From the time I regained curiosity and interests and hope and ambition, I've wanted to be better. But the change hasn't come. I'm yet to wow myself.
I'm sure many feel that way. We are all convinced of our potential but apparently dissatisfied with what we've made of it.

I've had conversations about this with many people for many years now. We speak of regret, the knowledge in our bones that we are better than this, a silent hope for things to change. I've seen no change. I've seen miraculous change.
I'm lucky enough to have been the recipient of the latter once before. Now I'm writing a blog, wondering if it's my time for the former or if I'm lucky enough to see some magic again.

So, is this a blog now? And I can just… put this out for anyone to discover and use to form an opinion about me? Scary. I should do this more often.
